- Recast the exact projector result as the normalized mean `2/3` plus the unnormalized integral `8*pi/3`.
- Introduced an open spectral-measure/line coefficient `Z_L(mu)`; `8*pi/3` is now explicitly a candidate convention, not a derived physical coupling.
- Reclassified `c_L = 0` as a preregistered subtraction convention.
- Added the logarithmic finite-core self-energy and retained genuinely nonlocal terms.
- Added the no-small-parameter obstruction for a tight trefoil, where `D*kappa = 2` on curvature-limited intervals.
- Added a naturalness table for representative curvature integrals.
- Made the self-linking/framing dependence explicit and added an illustrative framing-sensitivity table.
- Corrected the high-resolution provenance: `L_p`, `L_c`, and `L_a` are distinguished as polygonal estimator, upper bound, and weighted estimate.
- Quantified the 10.37 ppm branch separation versus the 855.86 ppm external residual.
- Added the Lorentz-universality gate to the gauge-matching hypothesis.
- Added direct prior art on magnetic-knot energy, vortex-knot energetics, and tight-flux-tube spectra.
- Reframed the paper as a negative/obstruction result rather than a near-derivation of a coupling.

No old exploratory archive papers are cited or used as scientific support. The article is self-contained and cites only the literature and supplementary data on which its arguments depend.
