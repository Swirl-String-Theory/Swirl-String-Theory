# Revision notes — manuscript v0.2.0

## Central correction

The original manuscript treated

\[
\frac{\rho_A}{\rho_B}
=
\frac{2\pi}{3}\frac{\ell}{r}
=
\frac{1}{2\alpha}
\]

as an exact geometric volume ratio. The algebraic ratio is correct for the declared closure coefficients, but the interpretation is not. The factors \(2\pi r^3\) and \((4\pi^2/3)r^2\ell\) are normalization supports generated by two energy formulas. They are not the Euclidean volumes of a sphere and circular tube.

The revised manuscript therefore labels this as a:

> conditional normalization identity, not a geometric volume theorem.

## Major revisions

1. **Density semantics fixed**
   - `rho_horn^eff` is retained as the canon-compatible horn-envelope normalization density.
   - `rho_fil^eff` is introduced as a separate effective line-energy coefficient.
   - Neither is identified with the background response density, substrate density, or resolved local-tube density.

2. **General coefficient dependence exposed**
   \[
   \frac{\rho_H}{\rho_F}
   =
   \frac{4\pi^2 C_F}{C_H}\frac{\ell}{r}.
   \]
   The factor \(1/(2\alpha)\) occurs only after choosing \(C_H=2\pi\), \(C_F=1/3\), and the calibrated electron scale chain.

3. **Ropelength gate added**
   - The ideal trefoil is usable only if the resolved physical energy is shown to be ropelength-dominated.
   - Soft field-theory solitons and hard fixed-thickness tubes are separated as different regimes.

4. **`h/m_e` demoted from assumed substrate quantum**
   - It is now called the electron-anchored Compton circulation scale.
   - The ratio
     \[
     \Gamma_{\rm eff}/\Gamma_C=\alpha^2/4
     \]
     is a calibrated comparison, not a derived dynamical deficit.

5. **Superluminal claim corrected**
   - Formal speeds above \(c\) do not contradict Galilean incompressible Euler theory by themselves.
   - They obstruct a literal universal-\(c\) relativistic interpretation of the same transport velocity.

6. **Self-energy matching corrected**
   - A general electromagnetic shape factor is included:
     \[
     E_{\rm EM}
     =
     C_{\rm EM}\frac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0D}.
     \]
   - Hence \(D=r_e\) follows only when \(C_{\rm EM}=1\).
   - “Equipartition” is replaced by “equal-self-energy matching ansatz.”

7. **Maxwell bridge decomposed**
   - B2a: Maxwell-form infrared action.
   - B2b: source/charge normalization.
   - B2c: equality of vortex response and bulk electromagnetic stiffness.

8. **Overclaim about one free parameter removed**
   The physical model still has several open quantities: the dynamically selected knot, line-energy coefficient, density-sector mapping, circulation law, electromagnetic response match, and charge-profile shape factor.
