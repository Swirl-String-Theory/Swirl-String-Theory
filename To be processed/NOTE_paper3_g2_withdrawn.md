# Note — Paper 3 (g = 2 as one quantum of circulation): withdrawn before drafting

Date: 3 August 2026
Status: **cancelled.** Not drafted, not to be revived as a standalone paper.

## What was proposed

A two-page note claiming an α-free kinematic anchor: for a charge circulating on a
loop of radius `R` at speed `v`,

    g = 2 m v R / hbar,    hence   g = 2  <=>  v R = hbar/m,

with the unique subluminal solution `R = lambdabar_C`, `v = c`. This was to be the
one result in the programme that consumes no α and could therefore legitimately
feed the fine-structure work.

## Why it was cancelled

**1. The derivation as I first stated it was wrong.**

For a single classical current ring, magnetic moment and mechanical angular
momentum are

    mu = q v R / 2,     L = m v R,     so   mu / L = q / (2m)   always.

Therefore **g ≡ 1** for any ring, independent of `R` and `v`. This is the standard
orbital g-factor. My formula `g = 2mvR/hbar` silently used `S = hbar/2` in the
denominator while using `S = mvR` in the numerator — two different values of the
same quantity in one equation.

The formula is recoverable only under an extra assumption: that the electron
carries intrinsic spin `hbar/2` which is *not* identified with the mechanical
angular momentum of the charge loop. That is a model assumption, not kinematics.
The claim "kinematically exact, α-free" was therefore overstated.

**2. Even repaired, the result is Huang 1952.**

K. Huang, *On the Zitterbewegung of the Dirac Electron*, Am. J. Phys. **20**(8),
479–484 (1952), doi:10.1119/1.1933296, shows that the zitterbewegung can be viewed
as circular motion about the spin direction with radius equal to the reduced
Compton wavelength, that the intrinsic spin is the orbital angular momentum of
that motion, and that the associated current produces the intrinsic magnetic
moment. That is the entire content of the proposed note, in a teaching journal,
seventy-four years ago.

The factor-of-two structure is likewise standard: see M. Rivas, *On the kinematics
of the centre of charge of a spinning particle* (arXiv:0807.2512), where the total
spin is half the zitterbewegung angular momentum, which is exactly why expressing
the moment in terms of the total spin yields `g = 2`.

**3. The configuration is excluded anyway.**

`lambdabar_C = 386 fm`, against the electron-magnetic-moment substructure bound of
roughly `4 x 10^-19 m`. Nine orders of magnitude. As a literal charge loop the
configuration is dead, by the same argument already applied elsewhere in this
programme.

## What survives

One sentence, as a remark inside a future paper — not as a paper:

> Under the standard zitterbewegung identification, `g = 2` states that the charge
> streamline carries exactly one circulation quantum `h/m_e`, while the spin
> corresponds to `h/(2 m_e)` — in superfluid language, the unpaired and the paired
> quantum respectively.

**Status: `[OBSERVATION — not a mechanism]`.** The parallel with paired versus
unpaired circulation quanta is verbal. No dynamical account connects them.

Value: it gives a citable orthodox anchor (Huang 1952) for the Compton scale at
exactly the point where the vortex programme currently relies on its own
assumption. Worth half a page in the paper where that scale is used. Worth nothing
on its own.

## Process lesson

The prior-art check should have preceded the proposal, not followed it. The result
was proposed, accepted, scheduled, and only then searched. Three of the four items
that killed it (Huang, Rivas, the `g ≡ 1` algebra) were reachable in a single
literature search costing a few minutes, against a planned half-day of drafting
and an eventual desk rejection.

**Rule for the remaining items: search first, propose second.**
